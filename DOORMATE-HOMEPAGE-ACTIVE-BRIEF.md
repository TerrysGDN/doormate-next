# DoorMate Homepage — Active Brief and Completion Checklist

This file records Terry's current instructions for the DoorMate homepage recovery. It is the active working checklist, not permission to override Terry's latest instruction or verified business facts.

## Non-negotiable objective

Build a 10/10 modern evolution of DoorMate—not a copy of the 2016 website, not an AI template, and not a collection of disconnected patches.

DoorMate has traded since 2009 (16/17 years). Terry has more than 25 years of wider door-industry experience.

## DoorMate sales philosophy

- Make technically difficult sliding-door choices feel simple.
- The opening viewport is the shop window: authority, trust and the complete principal range.
- Menu links lead to explanatory product landing pages.
- Homepage product cards give purchase-ready customers direct buying routes.
- Lower sections each perform one distinct sales job; they must not repeat the same message or all behave like sales banners.
- Manufacturing supports authority. DoorMate has a genuine UK manufacturing advantage, but DoorMate is now a broader sliding-door systems company.
- DoorMate supplies its own products and carefully selected systems from established manufacturers.
- Do not reveal competitive kit-building or component-selection advantages.
- The website must communicate Terry's real experience and subtle sales thinking, not generic AI copy.

## Shared design system

- One continuous visual framework across every section.
- DoorMate core colours: navy, gold and white. Cream must not become a leading brand colour.
- Shared widths, spacing, heading scale, body scale, panels, controls and mobile behaviour must be enforced centrally.
- Design decisions must be checked in the rendered desktop and mobile site before being presented.
- Do not make Terry diagnose spacing, hierarchy, balance or individual pixels.

## Homepage section roles

### Opening viewport

- Establish "The UK's Leading Sliding Door Specialists" confidently.
- Show the complete principal range.
- Preserve Trading Since 2009.
- Show genuine trust proof.
- Product imagery remains partly placeholder and must be replaced with appropriate real imagery.

### Reviews

- Explain why independent reviews matter rather than shouting "look at our reviews".
- Preserve two genuine proof sources.
- Never show a failed or unavailable-review message to customers.
- Keep the mobile proof presentation compact.

### Manufacturing authority

- Preserve the approved human sales copy unless Terry requests a change.
- Present real UK manufacturing as evidence supporting the wider DoorMate range.
- Replace the placeholder manufacturing image with genuine DoorMate evidence.

### Relief from complexity

- Confusing terminology surrounds a clear central message.
- Core message: "You don't need to be an expert. We've already done the thinking for you."
- Use DoorMate navy, gold and white.
- The message—not the terminology—must dominate.
- "Real homes, Real doors, Real walls" must feel original and connected, not like three generic feature cards.
- The section must feel powerful and subtle, not like a template banner.

### Brands and final close

- The brand strip must use clean official logos with balanced visual weight and consistent spacing.
- It remains unfinished until the official artwork is prepared and visually checked.
- The homepage needs a quiet, polished conclusion—not another aggressive sales banner.
- Explain that DoorMate's own British-made hardware sits alongside carefully selected systems from established manufacturers.

## Future-facing discovery and commerce

- Build for accurate recommendation in ChatGPT shopping/search, Google AI features and Perplexity—not merely old keyword SEO.
- Maintain a clear machine-readable DoorMate identity, expertise, location and product catalogue.
- Product pages will need accurate Product/Offer/variant data, prices, availability, images and purchase routes.
- Prepare for Google Merchant Center and an OpenAI merchant product feed.
- Discuss the appropriate AI assistant and agentic-commerce/catalogue architecture before implementation.
- Never fabricate reviews, product claims, prices, availability, policies or business history for structured data.
